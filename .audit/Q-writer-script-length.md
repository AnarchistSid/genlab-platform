# Q — the writer produces 27 words where the kit wants ~70

**Filed** 2026-09-21, ANIME-12 §5. This is the finding reel A demonstrates.

The pipeline's own narration script for TOUGEN ANKI is 27 words. Rendered
through the STILL kit at the measured 177 wpm that yields:

    27 words -> 7.65s narration -> 2 shots -> 10.69s reel (with the 3s card)

against a 15-60s platform floor. The reel is COMPLETE and CORRECT and it is
still not publishable, purely on length. Reel B, from a 73-word script,
comes out at 27.62s and passes all eight gates.

So the writer's length is the binding constraint on the craft path, not the
renderer. Nothing in the kit can fix it: the kit can stretch 27 words over
more seconds only by holding stills longer, and the 4.0s longest-static rule
exists precisely to stop that.

Target: ~70 words, which is what a 5-beat story shape needs at 177 wpm to
land in the 24-28s band. Reel B's five beats are the shape to aim at.
